# Final design specification -- 1.40 m hand-launched powered glider

Single source of truth, generated by `final_analysis.py` (aerosandbox 4.2.10: VLM for NP / trim / lift distribution, AeroBuildup + NeuralFoil for drag and control derivatives; UIUC-calibrated propulsion model). Air rho 1.09 kg/m3, nu 1.7e-05 m2/s. Axes: origin = wing root leading edge, x aft, z up; all positions in mm. Design print density 0.75 g/cm3.

## 10. Top risks
| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landing: power-off glide from 10 m is 153 m at best L/D, longer than the 100 m field | Spoileron mix (-25 deg, glide 52 m from 10 m); start final at <= 6 m; S-turn on final; motor-off well before the threshold |
| 2 | Hand launch at 7 m/s needs CL 1.13 (98 % of CLmax 1.15) | Full throttle before release (T/W 1.12), throw >= 8 m/s, wings level, 5-10 deg nose up |
| 3 | PLA Aero density varies 0.65-0.85 -> AUW 573-633 g | Weigh each segment; calibrate flow/temperature first; re-place the battery for SM 12 % (table in section 3) |
| 4 | Turn width at 35 deg is 23.7 m (target 22 m) | Fly 40 deg bank (19.8 m, CLmax/CL 1.29) with FC bank limit 45 deg; turns may overfly the sidelines |
| 5 | NP uncertainty (VLM vs AeroBuildup disagree; flat-plate tail at Re ~ 40k) | Balance at 64 mm (SM 12 %); first flights at the forward end (SM 15 %, 59 mm); glide-test before powered flight |
| 6 | CD0 may be nearer 0.03 than the predicted 0.019 | Endurance margin is large (level current ~1.2 A); L/D would fall to ~13 |
| 7 | Mildly divergent spiral (t2 88 s) | Acceptable for an FC-stabilised aircraft; add 2 deg polyhedral if flown manually |
